Our Values

Teamwork

We unite community leaders, legislators, and youth individuals to build a collaborative shield against gun violence.

Factualness

We justify our policy proposals, advocacy, and educational materials through research-backed statistics and data-driven research.

Focus

We direct the entirety of our energy toward high-impact strategies aiming to empower survivors, while reducing community trauma.

Integrity

We operate with unwavering transparency, empathy, and a commitment to the safety and well-being of every individual.
